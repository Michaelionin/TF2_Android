# Team Fortress 2 on Android  
We will need:  
1) A powerful modern smartphone or tablet with Android 12 or higher  
2) A Steam account

## Procedure:  
First, download the GameHub app.  
**IMPORTANT!!! It was tested only on the latest official version. All older versions, modifications, and alternatives are at your own risk!**  
Once the app is installed, log in to your Steam account. **This is safe, the author of the instructions guarantees!**  
Next, enter “team fortress” in the app search and download our favorite shooter.  
**During the download, it’s advisable to make sure the gadget doesn’t go to sleep and doesn’t get disturbed unnecessarily.**  
Once the game has downloaded, don’t rush to launch it. It most likely won’t work correctly. And that’s when our...  
## Universal config comes to the rescue.  
Find the PC‑engine button in the game options. Go there, scroll through the tabs, and apply the settings as shown below:  
1) Game resolution: 800*600  
2) Disable window manager: ON  
3) Disable image quality improvement plugin: ON  
4) dxvk version: dxvk-1.10.3-async  
5) VRAM limit: 4096MB  
6) Compatibility layer: Proton9.0-x64-3  
7) CPU Box64-0.39 translator  
**Important! The choice of the graphics driver depends on the device.** In some cases, only the “System driver” is available, in which case you should select it. For Qualcomm, I recommend choosing the latest official qcom driver; artifacts have been observed on turnip.  
The config was tested on two devices from different manufacturers and with different CPUs. If you have tested it on your device and something doesn’t work, please report it.

## Tips  
1) If something isn’t working or you encounter any problems/questions, please report it immediately; we’ll do our best to take it into account and help.  
2) Set the game’s graphics settings to the minimum.  
3) Don’t try to replicate the experience on ultra‑budget smartphones.  
4) For Qualcomm devices, choose the proprietary qcom driver.  

## Bugs  
1) On some devices, depending on the video driver, artifacts may appear.  
2) On non‑flagship devices, low FPS may occur.  
3) Cloud synchronization may conflict.  
4) You cannot write in Russian in the chat correctly. To fix this, install Hacker's Keyboard.

## Successfully tested on devices:  
1) Redmi Note 13 Pro (average 6-16 FPS)  
2) Honor Pad 9 (19-30 FPS)
